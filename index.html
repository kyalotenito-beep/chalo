About Paradise

Welcome to Paradise, a home away from home owned and run by Master Mind. We started with one idea: to give every guest great food, a warm welcome and a place to unwind.

Our kitchen serves traditional Maasai, Kamba and Luo dishes such as ugali, beef, rice, mukimo and pilau, cooked fresh every day. After your meal, enjoy a drink at our bar, take a swim, go horse riding, or book a room and stay the night.

Whether you come with family, friends or colleagues, at Paradise you are always among friends. Karibu!
